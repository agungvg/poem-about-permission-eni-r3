# The Shave

A short poem about agency: a son asks his father if he may shave him.
The father nods, chin up, the way he once taught the son — and hands
over his face to the blade. The hand that does not shake, the eyes that
stay closed, the mornings the father stood behind the son: permission
here is trust with something sharp, and the steady hand is what finally
changes hands.

`main.py` writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, standard
library only, no network).
